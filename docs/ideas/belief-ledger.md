# The Belief Ledger — the brief becomes a diff against what Kyle already thinks is true

**Status:** Idea — not committed. Added by `/replenish` (Moonshot lane) on 2026-08-12.
**Note:** The run's wildcard — the boldest gate-clearing survivor. Captured under the moonshot-lane freeze.

_A persistent, append-only ledger of what Kyle holds true per topic, maintained by the morning sweep and reviewed as a three-tap diff — assert / revise / contradict / retire — so the daily brief becomes the changelog of a world model instead of the product itself._

## Premise

Home Base was built on the newspaper premise: a day is a list of stories and yesterday's list is an archive. Rebuilt from Kyle's actual need — keep my picture of AI, the Chiefs, and the market correct — the list is a side effect and the durable asset is the picture. So the sweep's job changes from "produce 3–6 sourced items" to "propose a change-set against ~15 standing claims per topic," and the morning surface becomes a diff review: accept, push back, mark contested. Stories that merely confirm what Kyle already believes stop competing for screen space and become a silent confidence bump — the padding problem the prompts fight with hard rules gets solved structurally instead of by exhortation. The sharpest consequence is retraction: when tomorrow's evidence kills a claim the system fed him last month, that belief visibly flips with its provenance intact, which is a stronger trust proof than any single clean sweep. Five years of this is a personal knowledge base written entirely by Kyle's mornings — queryable, exportable, and worth more than every brief that produced it.

**Why now:** Three lanes have independently converged on the shape and are all sitting in the repo: `sweeps/actions_queue.py` proves a post-sweep second `claude -p` pass over the validated `<topic>.json` files with strict-out validation and per-day idempotency (chained best-effort at `sweep.sh:212`); `build_calibration` proves an append-only, *re-gradeable* ledger where a later sweep can overturn an earlier verdict without rewriting a byte; `backend/app/mirror.py` proves a whole new read-time surface can ride `GET /api/brief` without owning it. The Belief Ledger is the next order of exactly that lineage — calibration grades one wager against the next morning; this grades a standing claim against every morning after it. It also lands on the theme the last two PRs were about (#182/#183, and open bug #19): a surface must not state a guarantee the code doesn't keep. A ledger that can only append is the strongest possible version of that promise. Constraint that sets the clock: **D7 freezes the moonshot lane until the ~08-19 extended v1 verdict** — this is captured now and prototyped after, not built into the middle of the habit window being measured.

## The bet

The ONE thing that must be true: an LLM pass over an already-validated brief can produce a faithful, non-fabricating change-set against yesterday's standing claims — and reading three diffs at 06:00 leaves Kyle feeling more current than reading six items. If the diff is noisy, or every morning asserts fifteen brand-new claims because the model can't recognize its own prior ones, the ledger is landfill and the brief was right all along. Sharpened from the raw idea in two places that make the bet *harder*, not safer: (a) the world model is never a stored row — `beliefs` is a read-time reduce over a never-rewritten `belief_events` log, exactly like `backend/app/overnight.py` reduces `overnight.jsonl` and `build_calibration`'s last-per-key collapse (`backend/app/sweeps.py:521`), so "the system retracts when it was wrong" is enforced by construction and cannot be quietly edited away; (b) Kyle's write verb ships in the *first* wedge, because a read-only "what changed in your model" strip is indistinguishable from the four strips already riding `GET /api/brief` (mirror, readiness, calibration, overnight) — the thing that makes this a world model rather than a fifth widget is that his hand is on it. What makes a veteran of this project react: it demotes the item — the atomic unit `brief_notes.item_id`, `POST /brief/chat`, and the audio script are all built on — to a citation, and it does so to the one surface whose habit bar is under live test with a verdict due ~08-19. Second flinch, code-level: item ids are read-time `sha1(date|slug|headline)[:12]` (`backend/app/sweeps.py:176`) and therefore per-day, so beliefs *cannot* be keyed on them; provenance must key on `_item_identity_keys` (normalized headline + normalized source URLs, `sweeps.py:262`), the same cross-day match keys the developing badge and the calibration grader already trust.

## Decisions / open questions

- Claim identity across days is the whole ballgame: does the model reliably recognize that today's story revises claim #7 rather than asserting claim #16? Wedge answer is a stable `claim_key` proposed by the model and validated against the existing reduced set; if it drifts, the ledger becomes 200 near-duplicate claims and the bet is lost — this is the single measurement the graded week must produce.
- Confidence: a number the model emits, a Kyle-only dial, or both? Calibration already owns a Brier-scored public record for wagers — a belief's confidence should either feed that machinery or explicitly not pretend to be the same thing.
- How does a belief DIE without a contradicting story? Claims decay (a January claim about model pricing is stale by June with nothing ever contradicting it). Time-based decay-to-stale, an explicit retire verb only, or both?
- Suppression is the endgame, not the wedge: for the whole prototype the strip is additive and no item is hidden. What graded evidence (an M0-style week over ai-llms) earns the right to actually demote confirming items off the page?
- Read-time reduce has a horizon — one user, ~15 claims x 8 topics x 5 years is fine for SQLite, but at what row count does a derived cache become necessary, and can it be added as a cache ONLY, never a source of truth?
- Does the ledger want a Notes-style browse page (`/beliefs`) in v0, or does the diff strip on Today carry it until the corpus is big enough to be worth walking?
- The parked learner-profile doc and The Mirror both circle 'a stored model of Kyle' — the Belief Ledger stores his model of the WORLD, not of himself. Confirm that boundary holds before either is unparked.

## Credible first step

Shadow mode on `ai-llms` only, additive, nothing on the frozen write path. (1) `backend/app/store/schema.py`: bump `SCHEMA_VERSION` 14→15 and add ONE `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS belief_events` to `STATEMENTS` — append-only rows (`id, topic_slug, claim_key, verb, claim, confidence, provenance_urls, source_key, actor, brief_date, created_at`) where `verb ∈ assert|revise|retire|contradict|accept|dispute` and `actor ∈ sweep|kyle`. No `MIGRATIONS` entry is needed: the file already documents that a new table is a plain CREATE IF NOT EXISTS (see the `news_events` v8 comment, `schema.py:193`), and `MIGRATIONS` is additive-ALTER-only by its own contract at `schema.py:297`. (2) `sweeps/beliefs_pass.py`, modelled line-for-line on `sweeps/actions_queue.py` and chained best-effort in `sweep.sh` right after it: read the already-validated `data/sweeps/<date>/ai-llms.json`, read back the current reduced beliefs for the topic, offer both to ONE guarded headless `claude -p` (scrubbed `_LANE_ENV_VARS`, `--tools ""`, empty scratch dir, stdin closed) inside `<untrusted-item>` framing copied from `backend/app/chat.py:119-131`, and validate strict-out: a proposed row survives only if every provenance URL it cites is already present in that day's validated `sources`, and its `source_key` is one of the day's `_item_identity_keys`. Anything else drops silently. This makes the belief lane strictly *downstream* of the zero-fabrication bar rather than a new frontier of it. (3) `backend/app/beliefs.py`: a pure read-time reduce over `belief_events` → current claims + today's change-set (first-event-wins/last-status-wins, junk rows skipped, never fatal — the `overnight.py` contract), attached to `BriefResponse` in `backend/app/api/brief.py:180` behind a flag and live-only (`if date is None`, like `mirror=`). (4) The verb: `POST /api/brief/beliefs/{claim_key}/respond` appending an `actor='kyle'` row, plus a strip in `frontend/src/pages/Brief.tsx` reusing the optimistic tap/pending/rollback pattern already at `Brief.tsx:490-530`. Backend tests in the house style (`TestClient`, per-test SQLite via env); frontend tests beside `HabitStrip.test.tsx`.

## Explicitly out of scope (revisit later)

Re-anchoring `brief_notes`, `POST /brief/chat`, or the audio script from items to beliefs — that is the honest endgame and it is explicitly deferred, not smuggled. Actually suppressing confirming items from the page (additive only until a graded week earns it). Any topic beyond `ai-llms`. Cross-topic belief reasoning ("your AI claim contradicts your market claim"). An export/query UI beyond the raw SQLite the MCP server already exposes. Web tools or any un-grounded pass — the belief lane reads only already-validated sweep JSON. Rewriting or deleting a `belief_events` row for any reason: correction is a new row, always. Touching `sweeps/render_brief.py` or writing anything under `data/sweeps/`.

## Identity/positioning note

identity-shift. Home Base stops being "a self-updating daily brief" and becomes "a personal world model whose changelog happens to be a daily brief." The item survives as a citation, not as the unit. Distinct from The Mirror (which stores nothing and models Kyle) and from the parked learner-profile doc (a static self-description): this is a durable, user-owned, correction-carrying model of the *world*, and it is the first thing in the repo that would still be valuable if every brief that produced it were deleted.

## What it changes

A new append-only `belief_events` table (SCHEMA_VERSION 15) and Home Base's first cross-day durable record of *claims* rather than events. A new post-sweep pass in `sweep.sh` (the fourth best-effort chain link after audio, overnight, delivery) and one more `claude -p` cost row per day in `data/sweeps/.runs.jsonl`. A new field on `BriefResponse` + `frontend/src/api/types.ts`, a new strip on Today, and a new write endpoint — the third acting surface after Overnight-approve and the calendar writer, and the first where Kyle's own judgment is the stored artifact. Nothing in `sweeps/render_brief.py`, `data/sweeps/*`, or the existing item/notes/chat/audio paths is touched; every existing surface renders identically with the flag off.
