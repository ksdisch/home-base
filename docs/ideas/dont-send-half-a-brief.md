# Don't send a brief that isn't a brief yet

**Status:** Idea — not committed. Added by `/replenish` (Harden lane) on 2026-08-12.
**Note:** Merges two Harden survivors (Missing-Guardrail's send gate + Silent-Wrongness's truncated-MP3 check). Overlaps `delivery-admits-the-thin-day` (QuickWin) on the labeling half — build as one implementation if both are picked.

_One completeness gate in front of the outbound send — the MP3 must actually be as long as the script that produced it, and the day must cover enough of the active roster — so a half-failed morning is held for the catch-up instead of mailed, and any partial that does go out says "5 of 8 topics" in its own subject line._

## Premise

Email and iMessage are the only Home Base artifacts that can never be corrected. Everything else self-heals: the web brief re-renders when the on-wake catch-up fills in the late topics, and the page can be made honest at read time. The copy sitting in Kyle's inbox and the text on his lock screen cannot — there is no unsend. Today the outbound send has exactly one precondition (`brief.mp3` is a file, `deliver_brief.py:292`) and the audio has exactly one (`len(audio) >= 1000` bytes, `audio_brief.py:211`), so a three-of-eight morning and a Kokoro stream that died after topic two both mail out looking exactly like a quiet news day. This card is the merge of those two breaks into one guard with one authority: `audio_brief.py` measures what it actually produced and refuses to install a short render; `deliver_brief.py` gates the send on coverage and stamps the partials it does send; a small `brief.coverage.json` sidecar carries the facts between them and into the existing `brief-delivery.jsonl` ledger row. Best-effort stops meaning "send whatever exists" and starts meaning "may decline to send" — the only version of best-effort that is safe on an irreversible channel.

**Why now:** The delivery lane is eight days old (PRs #181/#183, live since 2026-08-12) and is the newest, least-hardened surface in the repo — it shipped with the weakest possible precondition on the strongest possible channel. It is also live *during* the extended v1 grading window (08-03 → 08-16, verdict ~08-19), on the exact days the supply side is known to wobble: the 08-11 hygiene brief found a weekly-quota wall recurring as Fri+Sat pairs, next due 08-14/15, inside the window. A three-topic email on a quota morning teaches Kyle that the brief is thin, not that the sweep broke — and that lesson lands in the one place the catch-up can never revise, while the habit is being scored.

## The bet

Three things must be true together. (1) Partial mornings are frequent and normal here — the 08-11 hygiene brief recorded 4 of 12 mornings failing, one with 6 of 8 topics dead while the heartbeat logged "ok" — so the gate fires on real days, not hypothetical ones. (2) A truncated-but-valid MP3 is a real failure mode, not paranoia: `render()` reads the whole body from a 240s-timeout urlopen and the only barrier is a 1000-byte floor, so a stream cut at topic two is atomically installed, mtime-marked fresh (so the on-wake re-fire is a no-op, `is_fresh` at line 195), narrated over by chapter chips computed from a words-per-minute estimate written BEFORE the render, emailed, and iMessaged as a tap-to-play link. (3) The completed brief arrives soon after — `_delivered_channels` (line 98) already keys on the mp3's mtime, so a regenerated brief re-sends once for free, which means the cost of holding is at most one delay, not a lost morning. A veteran of this project flinches because best-effort delivery has been sacred since M4 ("a down Kokoro must never fail a sweep") and this is the first pipeline step allowed to decline to deliver — into the one channel Kyle has watched work for exactly a week, right inside the graded habit window where a missing email reads as a broken feature. The steelman: a hold is not a drop (the sweep still exits 0, the web brief is untouched, the row is in the ledger), the hold is capped at one cycle so the brief always eventually lands, and an inbox that only ever contains real briefs is the entire trust story the ~08-19 verdict is measuring.

## Decisions / open questions

- Floor shape and default: a fraction of the non-paused roster (today 5 active of 8, since celtics / st-louis-blues / kansas-basketball are paused) or an absolute `min_topics` count? The fraction keeps a deliberate off-season pause from reading as a failure; ~0.6 keeps every existing delivery test green (their fixture is 1/1).
- Hold cap: one cycle (send unconditionally on the second invocation, ledger-detected) or a clock deadline ("send by 09:00 CT regardless")? One cycle needs no time logic and rides the ledger that already exists, but on a morning with no on-wake catch-up it could delay to the next day — decide before building.
- Duration ratio: 75% of the script-implied length, and what happens when the parser can't read the bytes at all (VBR, an unexpected container). Recommendation: fail OPEN on an unparseable file — install it, record `seconds_measured: null` — so the guard can only ever catch a file it positively knows is short.
- Whether the coverage floor should also refuse when audio coverage and page coverage disagree (`load_topics` silently skips unparseable topic JSON, so the MP3 can narrate six of eight while the page shows eight) or merely stamp that mismatch in the caption.
- Wording: "5 of 8 topics — 3 didn't run" in the subject vs a neutral "(partial)" tag; and whether the post-hold re-send is labelled an update so the second email isn't read as a duplicate.
- New sidecar `brief.coverage.json` vs extending `brief.chapters.json` — settled in favour of a new file, because the API parses chapters as a bare list (`backend/app/api/brief.py:95–118`) and would break on an object.

## Credible first step

Two files, both stdlib, no app/schema/frontend change. **Audio half — `sweeps/audio_brief.py`:** in `render()` (line 203) add a stdlib MPEG frame-header duration parser and compare the measured seconds against `words / WORDS_PER_MINUTE * 60` (WORDS_PER_MINUTE = 155, line 41); below ~75% skip the `os.replace` at line 215 (so the truncated file is never installed and never mtime-marks the day fresh) and raise — `main()` already returns 1 at line 281 and `sweep.sh:205` already tolerates and warns, so the never-fails-a-sweep contract is untouched. After a *successful* install, write `brief.coverage.json` beside the mp3 with `{topics_narrated, topics_expected, seconds_measured, seconds_expected}`, reusing the tmp + `os.replace` pattern already at lines 265–269. Keep `brief.chapters.json` exactly as-is — the API parses it as a list (`backend/app/api/brief.py:95–118`), so the new facts must not be folded into it. **Send half — `sweeps/deliver_brief.py`:** topics are already loaded at line 303, so the diff belongs there. **Correction to the input wedge:** `audio_brief.load_roster` (lines 49–70) does NOT carry `paused` — only the backend's loader does — so carry the flag through the dict it builds at lines 63–69 (`load_topics` reads only slug/title, so it's inert to both callers) rather than assuming it's already available. Gate the `pending` loop (lines 298–301) on a `min_coverage` key read from the same `load_config` (line 78) parse of `sweeps/delivery.json` (add it to the tracked `sweeps/delivery.example.json`); below the floor, `_append_row` (line 136) a `{"held": true, "covered": n, "expected": m}` row and return 0 — and hold at most once per day by checking the ledger for an existing held row, so the second invocation sends regardless. Above the floor but incomplete, thread the missing count into the subject at line 218 and `build_caption` at line 257. **Tests:** `backend/tests/test_deliver_brief.py` covers the send half with no network via the existing `_run` harness (line 73, `DELIVERY_SMTP_PASSWORD` + `DELIVERY_OSASCRIPT` stub); its fixture is 1 topic against a 1-entry non-paused roster, so a fractional default keeps all 13 green. `backend/tests/test_audio_brief_script.py` needs one new seam — it currently only points at an unroutable URL (`UNROUTABLE`, line 24) and has no Kokoro stub at all, so add a loopback `ThreadingHTTPServer` returning a hand-built short MPEG blob; `test_kokoro_down_exits_nonzero_and_leaves_no_file` (line 193) is the exact assertion template for "short render leaves the old file alone."

## Dependencies

`sweeps/audio_brief.py` (`render`, `load_roster`, `load_topics`, the chapters atomic-write pattern) · `sweeps/deliver_brief.py` (`load_config`, `_channel`, `_delivered_channels`, `_append_row`, `build_caption`, `send_email`) · `sweeps/delivery.json` (gitignored, PII) plus the tracked `sweeps/delivery.example.json` for the new key · the existing ledger `backend/data/brief-delivery.jsonl` · `sweep.sh:205` and `:220`, which already tolerate a non-zero exit from both scripts · `backend/tests/test_deliver_brief.py` and `backend/tests/test_audio_brief_script.py`. One genuinely new thing: a loopback stub-Kokoro HTTP server in the audio suite — no such stub exists today (the render tests only use an unroutable URL), so the short-render branch is currently untestable without it. No backend app change, no store schema, no frontend, no LLM, no network in tests.

## Explicitly out of scope (revisit later)

No retry or re-run of the topics that failed — the sweep's retry gap is a separate, larger piece of work. No in-app banner or page copy: the roster-minus-produced diff on Today belongs to `topic-didnt-run-banner`, and this card deliberately never touches the read-time surfaces. No push/alerting outside the app. No per-day historical record of which topics failed beyond the coverage numbers on the existing ledger row. No change to how the page renders a partial day, to `brief.chapters.json`'s schema, or to `GET /api/brief/audio`. No re-render or repair of a short MP3 — refuse and let the next invocation try again. No new config surface beyond the single floor key, and no UI for it.

## Identity/positioning note

Nothing changes in what Home Base IS, but one load-bearing assumption gets rewritten in place: assumption 2's "delivery is best-effort and never fails a sweep" currently reads as "send whatever exists," and this makes it read as "may decline to send, and never fails a sweep." That is the first time a Home Base pipeline step is allowed to withhold a deliverable rather than degrade it — worth recording as a decision, not smuggling in as an implementation detail.

## What it changes

The delivery pipeline gains the right to say no. Concretely: a below-floor morning produces no email and no text, one `held` ledger row, and a normal exit — the web brief and the sweep are untouched — and the completed brief goes out on the next invocation through the re-send path that already exists. A partial that does go out is labelled in the subject and the caption instead of impersonating a quiet day. A truncated MP3 is never installed, never mailed, never marked fresh, and never gets chapter chips asserting content past its own end. The delivery ledger gains coverage numbers, so a silent morning becomes an auditable one. Nothing about `sweeps/delivery.json`'s existing shape, the backend, the store, or any UI changes.
