# The Docket — retire topics, sweep open questions that can actually close

**Status:** Idea — not committed. Added by `/replenish` (Moonshot lane) on 2026-08-12.
**Note:** Convergence: two blind lenses (contrarian's "terminal topics" · constraint-killer). Complementary to the open `roster-entropy` idea — that one detects a cold roster, this restructures its unit. Captured under the moonshot-lane freeze.

_A second kind of roster entry — an open question with a written resolution condition — that the 06:00 sweep pursues across days and one morning closes with the evidence that closed it, so the brief's unit becomes something that can be finished instead of a subject that reports forever._

## Premise

Eight topics produce a brief every morning whether or not anything Kyle wanted to know actually changed. That is not a tuning problem — it is the unit: "AI / LLMs" and "Market & tech news" are faucets, not interests, and a faucet's only failure mode is becoming wallpaper. The honest-quiet-day language in every prompt and the whole roster-entropy premortem are symptoms of the same thing: a unit with no end condition. A question has one. "Does open-weights close the eval gap before Q1?" "Is the Chiefs' O-line fixed by week 4?" — each carries a written `resolves_when`, gets pursued across mornings, and one day drops off the docket into a closed-questions archive with the sources that settled it. The brief stops being a newspaper and becomes a case file that shrinks as it succeeds, and the docket's own length becomes the honest readout of how much Kyle currently doesn't know.

**Why now:** The moonshot lane is frozen until the ~08-19 verdict (D7), so this is captured now and built after — and it is precisely the thing to build if the verdict lands NOT YET a second time. The 08-03 check failed on phone-mornings and notes, not on trust: the brief was accurate and Kyle still didn't come. Roster-entropy (still open, detector never built) already named boredom as the real killer and proposed a badge; the Docket is the structural cure for the same diagnosis, and building the badge first would spend the subtract-affordance budget on a symptom. The rails are also at their warmest they will ever be: Calibrated Doubt just proved that an optional, leniently-parsed field can pass through the frozen renderer without weakening the sourcing bar, and `build_calibration` already grades yesterday's claims against today's items into an append-only ledger — a resolution is that exact shape.

## The bet

The one thing that must be true: Kyle comes back to a morning brief more reliably when it shows visible motion toward closing questions he asked than when it reports accurate news about permanent subjects — that narrative motion, not coverage or even accuracy, is what sustains the habit. This is a direct challenge to the project's named riskiest assumption ("sweeps stay accurate/trustworthy enough to sustain the morning habit"): the 08-03 v1 check ruled NOT YET on a week with zero fabrications, which is evidence that accuracy was never the binding constraint. What makes a veteran of this project react: the topic slug is the primary key of literally everything — the eight M0-graded prompts deliberately frozen for a year, `data/sweeps/<date>/<slug>.json`, the read-time `sha1(date|slug|headline)` item ids, every `brief_notes` row, the calibration ledger, the Mirror, archive search, audio chapters, and the scout's one-click add. This says the product's oldest primitive was scaffolding.

## Decisions / open questions

- Who writes the questions? v0 is hand-authored in sweeps/docket.json; the ambitious version has the sweep itself propose questions from what Kyle notes and asks — which reopens the assumption-4 grounding gate and should not be in the wedge.
- How is 'resolved' policed? The whole M0 thesis is that a claim needs a source; a resolution is a stronger claim than an item, so does it need a higher bar (two independent sources? a next-morning confirmation pass?) than inclusion does — or does Kyle confirm every close by tapping?
- One docket slug or one slug per question? One slug is the cheap wedge (single roster row, single prompt, single sweep call) but bounds how deeply any one question gets pursued; per-question slugs give each its own sweep budget and item ids at the cost of a roster that churns.
- Does a resolution belong in the calibration ledger? 'Closed on evidence' and 'wager landed' are close relatives, and build_calibration + backend/data/calibration.jsonl already carry the regradeable append-only pattern — reusing it vs. a separate closed-questions ledger is a real fork.
- Does the docket topic ride the audio brief and the email/iMessage delivery from day one? Motion on an open question is arguably the most listenable thing in the whole brief, but it means the wedge touches the delivery lane's word budget.

## Credible first step

One sitting, and it does not edit a single one of the eight prompts. (1) `sweeps/docket.json` — `[{id, question, resolves_when, opened, status}]`, seeded with three real questions. (2) `sweeps/docket.py` with two modes mirroring existing house patterns: `--stamp-prompt` renders `sweeps/prompts/docket.md` from `sweeps/docket.json` + a checked-in `_docket_template.md`, exactly as `backend/app/news.py:103 _write_topic_prompt` stamps a topic prompt from `_template.md` (note: the candidate's `_question.md` can never work — `sweep.sh` resolves `$PROMPTS_DIR/$topic.md` and `_template.md` states no roster slug may start with `_`); `--close --date` reads the day's rendered `resolution` fields and flips `status` in `docket.json`. (3) Append ONE roster row to `sweeps/topics.json` — `{"slug":"docket","title":"Docket","paused":false}`. Title it "Docket", single-token and inert: `backend/app/foryou.py:284 _roster_tokens` folds every roster slug/title token longer than two chars into the scout's coverage set and line 347 splits multi-word terms, so a row titled "Open questions" would silently suppress legitimate scout suggestions. That one row buys the entire pipeline for free — `sweep.sh`'s roster loop (lines 63–88; the file is at the **repo root**, not `sweeps/sweep.sh`), the cost ledger via `sweeps/envelope.py`, the atomic stage-dir write, read-time item ids, notes, `developing` labels, the audio brief, delivery, archive, and search all already key on a roster slug. (4) One lenient `_resolution` field (`no_movement | moved | resolved`, plus the sources that justify `resolved`) added to `normalize()` in `sweeps/render_brief.py` and hand-mirrored in `backend/app/sweeps.py:134` alongside `_wager_pair` — same contract: malformed degrades to no resolution and can never fail an otherwise-valid brief; `validate()` stays silent about it on purpose. (5) A pre-loop best-effort `python3 sweeps/docket.py --stamp-prompt` and a post-loop `--close`, in the same shape as the existing best-effort `audio_brief.py` / `actions_queue.py` / `deliver_brief.py` chain. `topics.json`'s eight existing rows, their prompts, and every topic-keyed `brief_notes` row are untouched. Then grade a fortnight of docket items against the same fortnight's topic items on the M0 rubric before proposing that any topic be retired.

## Dependencies

`sweep.sh` roster loop (repo root, lines 63–88) and `$PROMPTS_DIR/$topic.md` resolution · `sweeps/topics.json` (one appended row only) · a new `sweeps/docket.json` + `sweeps/docket.py` + `sweeps/prompts/_docket_template.md` → generated `sweeps/prompts/docket.md` · `sweeps/render_brief.py` `normalize()` field pass-through, hand-mirrored in `backend/app/sweeps.py` (the two `_wager_pair` copies are already kept in lockstep by hand — this adds a second such pair) · `backend/app/api/brief.py` + `BriefResponse`/`BriefItem` in `backend/app/models.py` for the docket + closed-questions payload · `frontend/src/pages/Brief.tsx` (the calibration strip ~line 809 and overnight section ~line 661 are the section pattern to copy) · `backend/app/foryou.py:284` roster-token coverage (constrains the roster row's title) · prior art to imitate, not depend on: `backend/app/news.py:103 _write_topic_prompt`, `sweeps/actions_queue.py`'s best-effort/idempotent chaining, `build_calibration`'s day-over-day join. Process dependency: the D7 moonshot freeze until the ~08-19 verdict, and an M0-style graded fortnight before any topic is retired.

## Explicitly out of scope (revisit later)

No edits to the eight M0-graded prompts, and no removal or re-keying of any existing roster slug, in the wedge — the docket runs alongside them and must beat them on a graded fortnight first. No migration of `brief_notes`, item ids, the calibration ledger, or archive search off the topic-slug key. No LLM-authored questions in v0 — Kyle writes them by hand. No auto-close: a `resolved` resolution is a proposal until Kyle confirms it, mirroring Overnight's draft-only rule. The sourcing bar is unchanged and unweakened; the docket adds a lane, it does not relax abstention. Not the roster-entropy cold-topic detector — that stays a separate idea, and if the Docket wins, it is what the detector should propose instead of a pause.

## What it changes

Today gains a Docket section that reads as a case file — each open question with its `resolves_when` and what moved on it this morning — plus a closed-questions archive that grows as the docket shrinks. The roster's lifecycle stops being active-vs-paused (its only lifecycle today) and becomes open-vs-closed, which finally gives the archive something worth searching and turns accumulated notes into evidence for a verdict rather than marginalia. Kyle gains one new verb: close a question with the evidence that closed it. If the graded fortnight wins, the frozen eight are no longer permanent — the roster becomes something a sweep can subtract from, in a project that today has enormous ADD machinery (scout, ten gate overrides) and zero SUBTRACT machinery.
