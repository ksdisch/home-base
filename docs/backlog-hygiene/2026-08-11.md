## 3. The findings that outrank the backlog

Verifying items against the repo meant reading live data. Two things are true inside the
graded window (08-03 → 08-16) whose verdict lands ~08-19.

### 3a. Supply is failing on two separable causes — neither is a backlog item

| Days | Result | Root cause (from the `.raw.txt` envelopes) |
|---|---|---|
| 07-31, 08-01, 08-07, 08-08 | **0 of 8 topics** | `"You've hit your weekly limit · resets 11am (America/Chicago)"` — $0 spent, 0 ms, 1 turn |
| 08-09 | 2 of 8 | `"API Error: Connection closed mid-response"` after real work |
| 08-10 | 5 of 8 | same |
| 08-11 | 8 of 8 | healthy |

The quota outages are **Friday+Saturday pairs exactly 7 days apart**. On that cadence the next
falls on **08-14/08-15 — inside the graded window.**

`sweep.sh` has **no retry of any kind** (`:189-195` copies the envelope to `.raw.txt` and moves
on) and does not distinguish *quota exhausted, retryable after the 11am reset* from *malformed
JSON, not retryable*. A retry after reset would convert a dark morning into a late-but-readable
one, which is what D12 actually counts.

**New defect in a shipped feature — failed-topic spend is never ledgered.** On 08-09 the ledger
records **$3.69**; the envelopes show **$12.38** actually spent:

```
2026-08-09  ledgered: st-louis-blues $1.86, fantasy-football $1.81, overnight $0.03  → $3.69
            unledgered failures: chiefs $1.98, market-tech-news $1.86, indiana $1.55,
                                 kansas-basketball $1.38, ai-llms $1.14, celtics $0.78 → $8.69
```

So PR #163's cost/health readout — whose idea doc calls the ledger _"the durable answer to what
do the sweeps cost"_ — under-reports by **70% on exactly the worst day**, and its `thin` flag
marks 08-09 as a *cheap* day when it was a near-full-price catastrophe that delivered 2 of 8
topics. The heartbeat has the matching blind spot: it detects **silence**, so it fired correctly
on the total outages (3 `ALERT` lines, flag auto-cleared on recovery) but logged
`"ok — newest ledger row 2h old"` on 08-09 with 6 of 8 topics dead. Liveness is instrumented;
**completeness is not.**

### 3b. Demand has already failed the bar it is being graded against

| Window | phone mornings (bar ≥5) | notes (bar ≥3) |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 · 08-03 → 08-09 | **0** | **1** |
| Week 2 · 08-10 → 08-11 (partial) | 1 | 0 |

Instrumentation is healthy — 98 visits recorded lifetime, attribution working since 07-27, and

phone on 3 of them; the **last note was written 08-03**. Counting `mac-localhost` generously as

Meanwhile the roster burns **~$19/day (~$132/week)**, flat at ~$2.35/topic regardless of season.

**The uncomfortable interaction:** D12's amendment holds that <5 readable **and** `mornings_phone`

The only clock-bound option. Cons: touches the trust-critical sweep lane (`render_brief.py` is
frozen), and perfects the input that isn't the one failing.

One subsystem, one theme; 50 existing path tests to land on; nothing frozen. The highest
integrity-per-line work in the corpus. Cons: improves a feature that the §3b data says nobody is

**Arc C — concurrency + honesty on shipped surfaces** · #16, #11. **← PICKED**
#16 can double a real, durable note on a phone double-tap and its docstring claims the opposite;
#11 blanks a whole dashboard when one of four calls fails, contradicting its own intent comment.
Both are small, safe, fully fenced, and both are cases of a shipped surface *stating a guarantee
it does not keep* — which is this project's stated core value. Blast radius:
`backend/app/api/brief.py`, `frontend/src/pages/Progress.tsx`, `frontend/src/pages/Brief.tsx`.
No frozen files, no external surfaces, no new dependencies.

**Arc D — demand, not supply** · `feed-the-vault-ritual` + `roster-entropy`.
Recommended at the gate on the grounds that PR10's gate had fired on its own stated terms and it
is the only arc aimed at the failing metric. **Not picked** — remains available and its
motivation is now recorded here.
