## What the paper found, and the instrument

The paper (*Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models*, Gurnee et al. 2026) claims large models carry a *global workspace*: a readable, steerable mid-layer scratchpad. They read it with a **Jacobian lens**.

Two primitives you must be able to define plainly:

- **Residual stream and unembedding.** The residual stream is the running "notepad" of vectors a transformer carries layer to layer; the unembedding turns a *final* vector into token probabilities. Read a *mid*-layer vector directly and you get garbage — it is not yet in output coordinates.
- **The Jacobian lens.** A per-layer linear "translator" (a transport matrix) that maps a mid-layer vector into the final layer's coordinates, so applying the unembedding after it reads out what the model is "currently thinking" in words.

## Your contribution: an open question, answered as a null

The paper proved the workspace at large scale (27B and up) and left small scale open. You rebuilt the lens from the spec and asked: does the workspace exist in *tiny* models (Qwen2.5 0.5B–3B)?

- **The bar (pre-registered).** Readability = pass@10 with a Wilson lower bound at or above 0.5 on at least 3 of 6 distributions. A lower bound is the honest thing to gate on.
- **The result.** A clean **null** — not readable (0/6 distributions at the bar on each model). Reported as the headline, with three downstream workspace properties (verbal report, swap, directed modulation) measured descriptively.

## The AGREE gate — why the null is about the models, not your lens

Before any measurement, the lens is validated **bit-for-bit against Anthropic's reference** (3220/3220 top-1 readout agreement). That is the AGREE gate. It proves your *instrument* is correct, so a 0/6 cannot be dismissed as "your lens is broken." What it does *not* prove is that your chosen *band* is in the right place (see below).

## The strongest positive

The 1.5B *introspection* result: steering a thought in (α=8) makes it reportable — **30/101** reportable versus an α=0 control at exactly **0/101**. A clean dose–response with the control pinned at zero is what makes it *causal*, not correlational.

## Decisions to defend

- **Band transplanted by percentile (38–92% depth), not re-derived at small scale.** Cheap and matches the paper, *but* a misplaced band could fake a null. Defense: the hybrid design (D2) — primary transplant plus secondary cheap per-layer diagnostics reported alongside, flagged where they only loosely agree.
- **Reference used as an oracle, not scaffold.** You get a bitwise correctness check without your measurements importing Anthropic's code — the same pattern as `lossy-wall`. A strength, not a shortcut.

## ⚠️ Common mistakes

- **Letting "null" sound like "it did not work."** The AGREE gate makes the null a statement about the *models*, not the instrument. Say that first.
- **Overclaiming the introspection positive.** It is one property on one model; report it as the cleanest *downstream* signal, not "the workspace is there."
- **Hiding the band risk.** The band placement is the honest threat; name it and point to the diagnostics you report next to the transplant.
